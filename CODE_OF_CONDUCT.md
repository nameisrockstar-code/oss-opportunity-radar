# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for all contributors. Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and personal attacks are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit, hide or reject abusive contributions or restrict participation. To report misconduct privately, contact repository maintainers using GitHub's private reporting options when configured; do not publicly disclose personal details. Maintainers will review reports fairly and promptly.
